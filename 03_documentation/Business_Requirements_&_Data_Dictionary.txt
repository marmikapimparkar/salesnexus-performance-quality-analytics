1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE & PROBLEM STATEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The objective of this project is to develop an end-to-end Sales Intelligence & Data Quality dashboard that enables business leaders to monitor revenue performance, customer behavior, operational efficiency, and enterprise data quality from a centralized Business Intelligence platform.

The organization generates sales across multiple products, categories, regions, and payment methods. Management requires a unified reporting solution to analyze revenue trends, customer insights, operational KPIs, and data quality issues to improve strategic decision-making and operational excellence.

The dashboard consolidates sales transactions, customer information, operational metrics, and data quality indicators into six analytical dashboards for executives and business managers.

The project answers critical business questions across six analytical areas:

Page 1: Executive Command Center

Page 2: Revenue Intelligence Hub

Page 3: Customer 360 Insights

Page 4: Operational Excellence Monitor

Page 5: Data Governance Center

Page 6: Strategic Decision Studio


2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs) & BUSINESS LOGIC
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The dashboard implements the following business KPIs using DAX.

* Total Revenue

Formula =
SUM(Revenue)

Business Purpose:
Measures the total revenue generated by the organization.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Total Orders

Formula =
COUNT(OrderID)

Business Purpose:
Measures the total number of customer orders.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Total Customers

Formula =
DISTINCTCOUNT(CustomerID)

Business Purpose:
Measures the number of unique customers.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Revenue Growth %

Formula =
((Current Period Revenue - Previous Period Revenue) / Previous Period Revenue) × 100

Business Purpose:
Tracks business growth over time.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Revenue MTD

Formula =
Month-to-Date Revenue

Business Purpose:
Measures revenue generated during the current month.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Revenue YTD

Formula =
Year-to-Date Revenue

Business Purpose:
Measures cumulative revenue generated during the current year.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Average Order Value

Formula =
Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders

Business Purpose:
Measures the average value generated from each customer order.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Revenue Per Customer

Formula =
Total Revenue ÷ Total Customers

Business Purpose:
Measures customer lifetime contribution to business revenue.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Orders Per Customer

Formula =
Total Orders ÷ Total Customers

Business Purpose:
Measures customer purchasing frequency.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Repeat Customer %

Formula =
(Repeat Customers ÷ Total Customers) × 100

Business Purpose:
Measures customer retention and loyalty.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Average Shipping Days

Formula =
AVERAGE(Shipping Days)

Business Purpose:
Measures average delivery duration.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Delayed Orders

Formula =
COUNT(Orders with Delayed Status)

Business Purpose:
Tracks delayed deliveries.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Delayed %

Formula =
(Delayed Orders ÷ Total Orders) × 100

Business Purpose:
Measures operational delivery performance.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Fulfillment Rate

Formula =
(Fulfilled Orders ÷ Total Orders) × 100

Business Purpose:
Measures successful order fulfillment.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Average Data Quality Score

Formula =
AVERAGE(Data Quality Score)

Business Purpose:
Measures the overall quality of enterprise data.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Invalid Emails

Formula =
COUNT(Invalid Email Records)

Business Purpose:
Identifies incorrect customer email records.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Invalid Phones

Formula =
COUNT(Invalid Phone Records)

Business Purpose:
Identifies incorrect customer phone records.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Revenue Mismatches

Formula =
COUNT(Revenue Mismatch Records)

Business Purpose:
Identifies revenue validation inconsistencies.

------------------------------------------------------------

* Duplicate Records Removed

Formula =
COUNT(Duplicate Customer Records Removed)

Business Purpose:
Measures data cleansing activities.


3. DATA DICTIONARY (CRITICAL METRICS EXPLAINED)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OrderID
Unique identifier assigned to every customer order.

CustomerID
Unique identifier assigned to every customer.

CustomerName
Customer full name.

Product
Purchased product.

Category
Product category.

Region
Sales region.

City
Customer city.

OrderDate
Date when the order was placed.

Revenue
Sales amount generated from the order.

Quantity
Number of units purchased.

UnitPrice
Price per unit.

PaymentMode
Payment method used by the customer.

CustomerSegment
Customer classification based on purchasing behavior.

OrderStatus
Current order status.

ShippingDays
Number of days required to deliver the order.

DataQualityScore
Overall quality score assigned to customer records.

ContactType
Contact validation category such as Valid Email, Valid Phone, Invalid Email, Invalid Phone and Missing Contact.

RevenueCheck
Revenue validation result (Valid, Mismatch or Missing).

IssueType
Business rule violation detected during data quality assessment.
